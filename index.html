How it works

No forms, no phone calls. Three steps, and the app does the rest.

  1. Take a photo

    Snap the problem. The app removes the photo's hidden location data before it leaves your phone.

  2. Pick what it is

    Choose a category. Your location and the street address are filled in for you; move the pin if needed.

  3. Follow it until it's fixed

    Watch the status change from submitted to fixed. Others nearby can tap "I see this too".
